Scan with your camera

Frames are resized and analyzed in the background, so the camera view stays smooth. A match only appears above 80% confidence.

Read it with no connection

Each landmark has its builder, its dates, a note on how it fared in the 2015 earthquake, and a gallery of 10 photos you can pinch to zoom. All of it is stored in the app.

Keep what you find

Bookmarks and your last 10 landmarks are saved on your phone. The app has no tracking and sends no usage data.

How it works, and the numbers behind it

A large EfficientNetB0 model was trained first. Its predictions were then used to teach a much smaller 4-block CNN (knowledge distillation, KL divergence loss, temperature 3.0). The small model is the one inside the app.

Per landmark on the validation set: Golden Gate 246 of 252 correct (F1 0.9572), Nyatapola Temple 196 of 220 correct (F1 0.9011). Latency was measured on one device, an OPPO CPH2413, after two warm-up runs. Real-world accuracy in poor light or crowds has not been measured yet.

The landmark text in the app was drafted from archaeological sources with Claude (Anthropic) and structured into monuments.json. Treat it as a guide and check dates against primary sources before you cite them.
